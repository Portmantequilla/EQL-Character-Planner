## ✦ What it does

The whole character, planned in one place — every number the same as the live game:

| Area | What you get |
|---|---|
| **⚔ Gear & upgrade tiers** | Every worn slot, item stats and tier upgrades, with an Optimal / Min-Max gear optimizer and a bulk tier slider. |
| **📜 Spell Manager** | Your spellbook with exact, client-mined damage / heal values, precise focus limits, and upgrade tiers. |
| **✧ Buffs & stacking** | Layer self, group, and external buffs — see what stacks, what's over-cap, and your real totals. |
| **❖ Pet builder** | Full pet paperdoll, per-slot gear, class-correct slot counts, and a survival-gear suggester. |
| **♜ Loot Filter forge** | Edit the game's AdvLoot `LF_*.ini` filters (Need / Greed / Never) without touching a text editor. |
| **☼ Macro library** | Build and manage your in-game socials / macros from a curated library, with live command validation. |
| **⚖ Stats engine** | HP, mana, attributes, resists — community-calibrated to the live game, with caps and over-cap flagged. |
| **✚ AA, skills & more** | Alternate advancement, skills, stances, and the rest of your character's dimensions. |

> *Too many tabs to count — the eight areas above plus per-character setup, an overview, and more.*

## 🛡 Built from the game's own data

Where the data was available, the numbers come straight from the client and the community's best
work rather than guesswork:

- **Spell damage, heals, and focus limits** are mined from the game client's own spell data.
- **Item tiers** follow the exact community-verified upgrade rule.

Plenty more is still being filled in — that's where the community comes in (see above).

## 💡 Suggestions & needed updates

**This is an active project, and your feedback shapes it.** If something's missing, wrong, or out
of date:

### → [Open a suggestion or report a needed update](../../issues/new/choose)

That link opens a quick guided form:

- **💡 Suggestion / Feature request** — a new feature, tab, or improvement you'd like to see.
- **🔧 Needed update / correction** — a stat, spell, item, tier, cap, or formula that doesn't match
  the live game (screenshots hugely appreciated).
- **🐛 Bug report** — something crashed or behaved wrong.

No GitHub account? You can also reach the project through [beecanyonretro.com](https://beecanyonretro.com).
Every report genuinely helps — the game keeps changing, and this is how the app keeps up.
